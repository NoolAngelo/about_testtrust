TestTrust

Monitor freely. Trust deeply.

A cheating-detection web extension for Chromium browsers that helps proctors and educators secure online exams, with no camera.

Rate TestTrust

Why TestTrust?

PrivateNo camera, microphone, or biometric data. It watches the browser, not the student.
Real-timeSuspicious activity reaches the proctor's dashboard instantly.
SimpleWorks on Chrome, Edge, and Opera. Easy for proctors to use.

How it works

1Student takes the examThe extension runs quietly in their browser.
2Events are sentSaved instantly to a secure cloud database.
3Proctor reviewsThe dashboard ranks students from Low to Critical risk.

What it notices

  • Tab switching
  • Window or app switching
  • Copy and paste
  • Idle time
  • Detached tabs
